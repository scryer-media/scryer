# Scryer Trademark and Brand Policy

Scryer's source code is free software under the [GNU General Public License v3.0](LICENSE). You may use, study, modify and redistribute it under those terms.

That licence covers the code. It does not cover the Scryer name and brand. The marks identify this project and its official builds, so that anyone can fork the software while users can still trust that something called "Scryer" comes from this project. GPL-3.0 section 7(e) permits this reservation.

## Marks covered

The following are trademarks of the Scryer project maintainers, whether or not registered:

- the names **Scryer** and **Scryer Media**
- the Scryer logo, icon and wordmark
- names of Scryer products and components that carry the Scryer name
- variations of the above that are likely to be confused with them

## Uses that need no permission

- **Referring to Scryer truthfully.** "Works with Scryer", "a plugin for Scryer", "built on Scryer", "a fork of Scryer". Use the name to refer to this project, not to brand yours.
- **Showing the Scryer logo in a project that works with Scryer**, for example to list Scryer as a supported application or on a "Connect to Scryer" button. Use the official files.
- **Redistributing official, unmodified builds** under the Scryer name, with all notices intact.
- **Running, modifying and self-hosting Scryer** for yourself or your organization.
- **Editorial and educational use**: articles, tutorials, talks and reviews.

## Uses that need permission

- Distributing a modified version under the Scryer name or logo.
- Publishing an application, container image, package or hosted service under the Scryer name, logo or a confusingly similar identity.
- Using the marks in a company, product, domain or account name in a way that reads as official, or that implies the Scryer project operates or endorses your offering.
- Merchandise, or using the logo to sell a product beyond a factual "works with Scryer" placement.

Never use the marks to impersonate the project or to mislead users about where software comes from.

## Naming your project

Give your plugin, client, tool or fork its own name, and mention Scryer in the description: "Lantern, a mobile client for Scryer". Repository and package names such as `scryer-plugin-example` are fine when "scryer" only describes what the code works with.

## Forks

You may fork Scryer; that is your right under the GPL. A distributed fork must not use the Scryer name or logo as its own identity. Choose your own name and logo, replace the Scryer brand files, and state factually that your project is based on Scryer.

## Brand files

The Scryer logo, icon and wordmark files in this repository are not licensed under the GPL. Projects that work with Scryer may include them to refer to Scryer as described above. A fork or modified redistribution must remove or replace them.

## Other projects' marks

Scryer shows the names and logos of services it works with, such as media servers, download clients, indexers and notification services, to identify them. Those marks belong to their owners. Their appearance in Scryer does not mean those projects endorse Scryer or are affiliated with it.
